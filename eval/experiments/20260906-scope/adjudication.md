# Final reserved-source evaluation protocol

The runtime was frozen at 031c48a6db078232b368bd8a9d3bbae42d066369efc60ae4ce6a9290e0b24a84
before reading the seeded source excerpts and writing these labels. There are
24 Korean and three English supported questions, eight insufficient controls,
and three under-specified requests. All twelve source groups are covered and
are canonical-target-disjoint from 210 + 39 + 38 development questions.

Purpose, residual-authority and short reference articles were retained from the
seeded sample. Questions include exceptions, timing, responsible actors,
conjunctions, contrary evidence and full requested lists. The three ambiguity
controls omit a trading method/quantity, an issue date, or a period unit and
applicable exception. Conditional comparisons elsewhere are supported questions.
One draft phrase was corrected to match the source's parenthetical wording
before grounding and before any retrieval. No labels were selected from search
results. Source/index grounding passed before the final pair.

The original starting executable and final executable run once each. Counts,
failures, source hashes, executable hashes and exit codes are retained. No code,
lexicon, weight, threshold or target changes follow these results in this cycle.

This is a small internal pilot, not an independent external audit. Related rule
families and translated provisions can overlap despite canonical source
separation. Three English supported questions do not certify broad English
quality. Status/nonempty clarification metrics are supplemented with manual
review of what missing information the clarification actually requests.
